Method

When does a possession change?

Each event tells us which team has the ball: shots, free throws, rebounds and turnovers. A new possession starts when that team changes, or when a quarter starts.

  • Made shot, last free throw made, or turnover → the ball goes to the other team.
  • Defensive rebound → new possession for the rebounding team.
  • Offensive rebound → same possession continues.
  • And-one and flagrant free throws stay in the same possession.
  • Team rebounds and shot-clock turnovers have no player; the team comes from the personId field.

Metrics

ORtg = 100 × Points ÷ Possessions
DRtg = opponent's ORtg
Net = ORtg − DRtg
Pace = average possessions × 40 ÷ minutes played

Source: official WNBA play-by-play (stats.wnba.com, PlayByPlayV3).
